Sub-hauler: story beats

The full campaign

  1. First delivery — keep the grow rooms alive. Carry filter supplies from Tidewall to Benthos. The first lamps come on, and Benthos asks for help restoring the pump and freight lift.
  2. Pump survey — find the fault. Inspect the pump at Pump Refuge. Its impeller is cracked; a replacement is available at Cormorant.
  3. Pump delivery — bring water back. Carry the replacement impeller from Cormorant to Benthos. Water returns to the grow rooms, and Pump Refuge becomes a working service berth.
  4. Lift survey — trace the breakdown. Inspect the cable housing at Freight Yard. The lift needs replacement cable from Benthos.
  5. Lift delivery — move freight again. Carry cable reels from Benthos to Cormorant. The lift and workshops return to work, and Lower Gantry becomes a service berth.
  6. Beacon delivery — reconnect Deep Anchor. Once the pump and lift are restored and the sub has a pressure seal, carry the assembled beacon core from Cormorant to Deep Anchor. Its beacon and inhabited capsule lights come on.
  7. Homecoming — bring the first reply home. Carry Deep Anchor’s network dispatch to Tidewall. Tidewall’s signal lights complete the network; the voyage ends with a homecoming and the option to keep exploring.

The pump and lift branches can be completed in either order. Their surveys can be recorded early. These are the existing campaign objectives; each can eventually have a short sequence of story cards.
