SUB-HAULER

trim sandbox · one hull across pilot, loading & fleet

boot

CONTROLS

  • ←/→ or A/D — thrust / reverse
  • ↑/↓ or W/S — blow / flood ballast
  • 1/2/3 — switch MINNOW / HAULER / LEVIATHAN
  • E — dock / undock at a station
  • ↑/↓ — select a station or shop row
  • Z — A: select / accept / buy in menus
  • X — B: back / undock in menus; abandon is a row
  • Space — sonar ping while piloting (costs air)
  • M — chart (pauses)
  • C — toggle current
  • R reset · N new cave
  • G — debug: reveal the whole chart

INSTRUMENTS

  • Left gauge = ballast. Above the notch: heavy, sinks. Below: light, rises.
  • The notch moves with cargo. Adding cargo makes you heavy until re-trimmed.
  • Top-left bar = air. Time, sonar, and blowing spend it.
  • Top-right pips = hull.
  • Dock frame = settlement. Stop inside it and press E.
  • M chart shows explored water and known settlements. Sonar reveals a larger area.
  • Chart crosses: TIDEWALL NW, BENTHOS N, CORMORANT E, DEEP ANCHOR S.
  • Job codes: TIDE, BENT, CORM, DEEP.
  • Station board and shop are 128 × 64 1-bit screens with a 5 × 7 bitmap font.
  • Menus use A/B labels; browser keys are Z/X as in Ardens.

SCOPE

  • 2,560 × 1,280 cave, four settlements, ring tunnels and side branches.
  • Dock to refill air, repair, take contracts, and buy upgrades.
  • Station board and shop use the device screen budget: 128 × 64, 1-bit, 5 × 7 font.
  • No finish or broader economy yet.
  • Find the trim: stop, hover, cross a tunnel, re-trim after cargo.
  • Flow: dock → accept a contract → pack the hold → LOAD & UNDOCK → deliver.
  • The selected hull is shared by PILOT, the loading screen, and FLEET. Cargo rides inside the hull; it is no longer stacked above it.

LOAD CARGO

selected hull · pack the hold

boot

GOAL

  • Packing opens automatically after you accept a station contract. Pack enough for the contract's crates; bonus pieces fill the hold.
  • Crate shapes are randomized for each load. Press N to roll a new cargo set.
  • MINNOW: 6×3 hex hold, 3 required + 1 bonus, 12/14 cells.
  • HAULER: 11×3 split/notched hold, 6 required + 1 bonus, 26/28 cells.
  • LEVIATHAN: 14×4 twin-spine hold, 6 required + 2 bonus, 40/44 cells.
  • Lower crates manually. Slide them sideways or up to tuck under overhangs.
  • Release only when the bottom touches cargo or the floor. No floating.
  • Installed crates stay in the tray as hollow icons. Select one and press A to lift it back onto the crane.
  • Cargo directly above blocks lifting; clear the top layer first.
  • No timer. Undo and rearrange until it fits.
  • LOAD & UNDOCK checks the contract requirement, sends the packed hold into the pilot cargo/trim model, and returns to travel. CANCEL or Esc returns to the station.

CONTROLS

  • 1/2/3 or the hull buttons — switch hull
  • ←/→ — select tray crate, or slide the suspended crate
  • ↑/↓ — lower / raise the suspended crate
  • Z — A: pick up / release (needs support below); select installed tray icons to rearrange
  • X — B: rotate suspended crate; with no crate, undo last
  • R — reset the current hold · N — new random cargo
  • Enter / L — load & undock · Esc — cancel

NOTES

  • Piece sets scale with the hull: 4 pieces / 7 pieces / 8 pieces.
  • Shapes are random each load; switching hull also rolls a new load.
  • The hold screen is 128 × 64, 1-bit, 5×7 bitmap text.
  • The hold is a compartment inside the pressure hull: cells scale 7/6/5 px per class so the bow cabin, engine room, and ballast stay visible. No scrolling.
  • Blocked cells are structural and solid: cargo cannot enter the HAULER split bulkhead or the corner notches.
  • Pilot time, air, and the contract clock are paused while loading.
